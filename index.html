Introduction

CORTIS is a five-member boy group from BIGHIT MUSIC, the first new group from the label since TOMORROW X TOGETHER. They call themselves a “young creator crew” because every member takes part in making their music, choreography and videos.

They debuted on August 18, 2025 with the single “What You Want,” and their first EP, COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES, followed on September 8, 2025.

Music

COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES (2025)

The debut EP opens with “GO!”, which all members helped write and compose and whose music video they co-directed. The title track “What You Want” blends psychedelic rock guitar riffs with hip hop. “FaSHioN” is another standout track with its own music video.

Second mini album

The group's second mini album includes the title track “REDRED” along with “ACAI” and “TNT,” and its music videos passed 10 million YouTube views faster than their debut ones.
